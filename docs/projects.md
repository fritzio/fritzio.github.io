        <div class="project-dates">2025 &ndash; <strong class="text-accent">Present</strong></div>

    <div class="project-description">

I am the main contributor and maintainer of the framework.

                LAMAS
            </a>
        </h3>
        <div class="project-dates">2019 &ndash; 2026</div>
    </div>
    <div class="project-description">
        LAMAS is a GPU-acclerated simulation framework for laser-based advanced manufacturing technologies, primarily focused on laser powder bed fusion. The mesoscale is resolved by SPH, and the laser beam is modeled using a ray-tracing algorithm. LAMAS aims to deepen our understanding of high-energy laser–matter interactions and their effects on process outcomes. I am the main contributor and maintainer of the framework.
    </div>
